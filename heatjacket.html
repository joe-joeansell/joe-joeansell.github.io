Media4Me - Figma Mobile App Prototype

2026 University Project. Figma prototype and design/planning concepts for a smart IoT heated jacket.

Figma | Internet Of Things | Interaction Design | App Design | Market Research

"Heat" was the result of a project where I had to create or conceptualise a smart IoT device, along with an accompanying app or web portal. This project tested my Figma skills, my project management and market research skills and my ability to consider both web design and practical interaction design.

I had to consider all aspects of the device. I had to understand IoT protocols, research and evaluate competitors, consider hardware cost and accessibility, how the device would look and function and potential risks- all whilst designing an app prototype with these same considerations.

This was all ultimately laid out in a slideshow which presented my findings. You can find excerpts of it below.
